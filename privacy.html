HelioForm Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 September 2026

HelioForm is developed by Maksymilian Jusinski ("we", "us"). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the iOS game, contact support, or visit this website. For privacy questions, email borych37@gmail.com.

Playing the game

HelioForm does not require an account. The app does not send your personal information or gameplay activity to us. It has no advertising, tracking, or third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.

Game progress, ramp designs, scores, and settings are saved on your device so you can continue playing. We do not receive these saves or operate a cloud service for them. Your operating system may include app data in device backups, depending on your backup settings.

In-app purchases

Apple processes the optional Full Game Unlock purchase through the App Store. The app uses purchase information supplied by Apple on your device to unlock content and restore purchases. It does not upload that information to a server operated by us. We do not receive your payment card details or Apple Account password.

Apple's handling of purchases, App Store activity, and any diagnostics it collects is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy and your Apple privacy settings.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and anything you choose to include, such as a description of an issue, screenshots, or device details. Providing this information is optional. We use it to answer your request and investigate the issue. Please avoid including passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Maksymilian Jusinski is responsible for this support information. Where European data protection law applies, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to requests and providing support. We keep correspondence only as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up, or to meet applicable legal obligations. We do not use it for advertising or sell it.

Our support mailbox uses Gmail. Email providers process and store messages to deliver the service and may process them outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. See Google's Privacy Policy for its processing and international transfer safeguards.

This website

This support and privacy website is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics, tracking scripts, or advertising cookies. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. GitHub may process website information internationally, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Visiting this website is separate from playing the game.

Your choices and rights

Deleting the app and its data removes its local saves from that device. Offloading the app can retain that data. Device backups and Apple's purchase records are managed separately through Apple; deleting local saves does not cancel an App Store purchase.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of support information you have sent us, email borych37@gmail.com. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, obtain portable data where applicable, and complain to your data protection authority. We do not use support information for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects.

Children's privacy

The game does not request personal information from children. If a child has sent personal information to our support address, a parent or guardian can contact us to request its deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our privacy practices change. The date above identifies the latest revision.
